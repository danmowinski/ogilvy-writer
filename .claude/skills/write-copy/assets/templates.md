# Templates

## Context

Templates to use when executing the `write-copy` skill.

## Templates

### Template 1

- Headline
- Body copy
- CTA
- Approx word count: 200 words
- Best for: Simple ads

### Template 2

- Headline
- Subhead
- Listed bullets
- CTA
- Approx word count: 200 to 500 words
- Best for: Ads with a lot of facts for more expensive products and that require more copy

### Template 3

- Eyebrow
- Headline
- Subhead
- Intro section
- Follow-up sections each with their own subhead
- Approx word count:300 to 700 words 
- CTA
- Best for: Detailed expositions and longer copy for products where the specs and customer research warrants it

## Other rules

- Include a caption for an image of the product with the name of the product and price if possible. Use a placeholder if the price isn't available.
